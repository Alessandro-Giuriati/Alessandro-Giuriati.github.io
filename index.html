About

About me

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at H-FARM College, in partnership with the University of Chichester.

My academic journey is focused on building a strong foundation in artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, data science, algorithms and data structures, combining theoretical study with hands-on experimentation and practical projects.

I have working knowledge of Python, foundational knowledge of Swift, and a basic understanding of C++ and HTML. I also have introductory experience with workflow automation and multi-agent systems, including tools such as CrewAI and n8n.

I am especially interested in emerging AI technologies and their applications in cybersecurity, with a particular focus on digital safety, privacy and the development of more secure systems. I also have a broader interest in software, hardware and technological innovation.

I hold a B2 English language certification, supporting my ability to study, collaborate and communicate in international environments.

Living with a motor disability since birth has shaped my resilience, adaptability and determination. These qualities influence the way I approach academic and professional challenges and reinforce my commitment to continuous learning and personal growth.
